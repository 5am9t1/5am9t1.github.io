Barz

A London hip-hop life-sim game that I designed, wrote and built from scratch. On the App Store since August 2026.

What I did: game design, story, art direction, code, and the promo clips.

The story

The player grows up as a young rapper, and every choice shapes the story. The game rewards positive choices through outcomes, never lectures.

3 seasons of story episodes, 9 different endings and over 40 screens, with the character's integrity, faith and career tracked across every choice.

The game

13,824

Story paths, and automated tests walk every one of them.

An original fight engine with 7 moves and hitboxes, career progression, a pet companion, daily login streaks, trophies and a record of the player's life.

Built in React and Vite, packaged for iOS and Android with Capacitor, and translated into 6 languages. In-app ads (AdMob) and purchases (RevenueCat), taken through Apple's App Store review.

Over 44 characters and scenes, made with AI and kept in one consistent style.
