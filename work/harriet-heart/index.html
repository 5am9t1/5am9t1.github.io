Harriet Heart

An original character I have been building since 2001, with a city and a crew of her own.

What I did: the character design, the story world, the art direction, the logo and the website.

Visit harrietheart.com

Tap any picture to see it full size.

Who she is

Harriet Heart is a pop star and producer in Vellacity, a city where everything hovers.

Her sound is Electro Hop: 80s hip-hop, pop, dance, R&B and Afropop, made at home and tested on the roofs. When something called the Hush starts turning the city down, she turns it back up, one beat at a time.

Out at night in her dad's cab.

The crew

8 friends, and each one brings a colour of their own.

I designed every one of them to stand next to Harriet Heart in the same world, from her twin Hunter to Sir-Rio, the robot who folds into a boom box.

  • Hunter

    Her twin, and he always wants the last word.

  • Zeienka

    Green, head to toe. Never, ever messy.

  • Daisuke

    Builds things. Some of them even work.

  • Asher

    Best piano player on their street.

  • Taaliah

    Keeps her word, even when nobody is watching.

  • JJ

    Street dancer, with the best pop-lock in Vellacity.

  • Rory

    Runs the rooftop station, and says when the kick is late.

  • Sir-Rio

    Shy, so around strangers he folds into a boom box.

The website

harrietheart.com is built by hand to launch her debut single, Just a little love.

In one scroll scene, paint bursts give the colour back to the city, and every burst lands on the beat of the song. A button plays the beat as well, made in the browser with no audio file.
