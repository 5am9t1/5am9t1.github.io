Chris Allen Jewellery

A one-off pendant, from an idea told out loud to a piece you can wear.

What I did: 3D modelling and rendering in Blender, and the promotional reel.

Tap any tile to see it full size.

The brief

Chris described a serpent coiled around a geodesic sphere.

I modelled it in Blender, from the first block-out to the lit beauty render, so the piece could be judged and changed before any metal was cut.

The finished piece
