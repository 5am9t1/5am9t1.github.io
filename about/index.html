The machine behind it

Everything I make now runs on MissionControl, the AI operating system I built for my own work.

It holds over 200 reusable skills, specialist agents that work in parallel, memory that carries from one session to the next, scheduled jobs, and quality gates that stop weak work before it is published.

The production line on the home page has made over 250 videos for NeuroLoop 100 and DailyPrayersFirst, with no manual step per video.

  • Claude connected to Shopify, TradingView, DaVinci Resolve, Ableton and Blender through MCP servers.
  • Consistent characters across AI image, video, voice and music.
  • SEO and GEO audits that make brands visible in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.
  • Claude Code agents, skills and memory, built for my own businesses and ready for yours.
