Privacy notice

What this site collects, why, who else sees it, and how to have it changed or deleted.

In effect from 18 November 2026. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Sam Dedewo (Wilfred Samson Dedewo), a sole trader in the United Kingdom who trades as 5AM9T1. I decide what happens to the information described here. To ask anything about it, email sam@5am9t1.com.

The short version

  • The only personal information I ask for is what you type into the contact form or send me by email.
  • I use it to reply to you and, if you ask, to send you a quote. I do not add you to a mailing list.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking on this site. The one exception is YouTube, and only if you press play on a video.
  • I do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

What I collect, why, and the legal basis

When you use the contact form or email me

The form asks for your name, your email address, the kind of enquiry, what you need, your budget, when you need it, and your message. The form emails these to my inbox, which my web host also runs. The website itself keeps no copy.

I use them to reply to you and to prepare a quote when you ask for one. If you ask for a quote, the legal basis is that you asked me to take steps before we might agree a contract. For other messages, the legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering people who write to me.

No law requires you to give me this information. Without your name, email address and message, I cannot reply.

Protection against spam

To stop automated spam, the server allows each visitor 5 messages an hour. To count them, it keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address with the times you sent a message. The record holds no readable IP address, and times older than 1 hour are ignored. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in keeping the form usable and free of spam.

Server logs

Like every web host, my host records technical details of each visit in its server logs: your IP address, your browser, the page you asked for and the time. These logs keep the site running and secure. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a working, secure website. My host keeps them under its own rules, which its privacy notice sets out.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. When you send the form, my host's bot check can set 1 cookie whose name starts with humans_. It tells people apart from bots, and the form needs it to work. Because it is strictly necessary, it does not need your consent.

Videos

Videos on this site come from YouTube, in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. When you do, Google receives your IP address and can store information on your device, as described in Google's privacy policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to social networks, app stores and my clients' websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Nothing is sent to them until you follow a link.

Who else handles your information

  • Bluehost (Newfold Digital, Inc., United States) hosts this website, the contact form and my email inbox.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts a copy of these pages at 5am9t1.github.io. That copy has no working contact form.
  • Google (YouTube), only if you play a video.

No one else receives your information from me, unless the law requires it.

Information sent outside the UK

Bluehost's servers are in the United States, so your message is stored there. Newfold Digital states that it relies on standard contractual clauses for information transferred from the UK; see its UK privacy addendum. GitHub and Google explain their own transfer safeguards in their privacy notices.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: deleted within 24 months of your last message.
  • Messages with clients: kept for 6 years after the work ends, for tax and contract records.
  • The spam record: not used after 1 hour.

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of the information I hold about you, and ask me to correct it, delete it, limit how I use it, or send it to you in a format another service can read. You can also object to my using it on the basis of legitimate interests. Email sam@5am9t1.com. I reply within 1 month.

I make no automated decisions about you and build no profile of you.

If you are unhappy with how I handle your information, please tell me first so that I can fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children

The contact form is for people who want to ask about paid work. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to get in touch for you.

Changes to this notice

If what this site collects changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
